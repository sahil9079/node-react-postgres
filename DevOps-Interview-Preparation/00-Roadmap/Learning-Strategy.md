# 90-Day Learning Strategy & Study Methodology
*Framework for Retaining, Implementing, and Defending DevOps Systems*

---

## 1. The Daily 10-Step Mastery Loop

Every topic is studied through this strict iterative cycle:
```
1. LEARN        --> Understand the core architectural definition and why it exists.
2. UNDERSTAND   --> Trace low-level component interactions and internal data flows.
3. EXPLAIN      --> Deliver a concise verbal definition in plain English.
4. PRACTICE     --> Build clean configuration files and manifests from scratch.
5. BUILD        --> Deploy working services in a sandbox environment.
6. BREAK        --> Intentionally inject realistic faults (misconfigurations, port mismatches).
7. TROUBLESHOOT --> Follow standard SRE triage workflows to identify root cause and fix.
8. DOCUMENT     --> Record the exact symptoms, commands, logs, and remediation steps.
9. INTERVIEW    --> Answer rapid-fire, scenario-based, and cross-examination questions.
10. SPEAK       --> Deliver a confident, professional 2-minute architectural explanation.
```

---

## 2. Daily Time Allocation Rule (8–10 Hours)

* **30% Theory & Architecture:** First-principles understanding, internal mechanics, protocol design.
* **40% Hands-On Building:** CLI mastery, YAML manifest authoring, Dockerfiles, Terraform scripts.
* **15% Failure Injection & Troubleshooting:** Simulating broken states, debugging logs, repairing clusters.
* **10% Interview Q&A Drills:** Mock questions, cross-questioning defense, trade-off evaluations.
* **5% Professional English & Documentation:** Spoken fluency, architectural summaries, RCA writing.
